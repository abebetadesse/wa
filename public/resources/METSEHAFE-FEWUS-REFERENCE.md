## 2. What Is Metsehafe Fewus?

*Metsehafe Fewus* (መጽሐፈ ፈውስ) is a category of **Ge'ez-language Däbtära healing
compendium** — a corpus of sacred medical, liturgical, and astrological knowledge
maintained by the *Däbtära* tradition of the Ethiopian Orthodox Täwahedo Church.
Unlike ordained priests (*qäsis*, ቄስ), Däbtäroch (ደብተሮች) are unordained
ecclesiastical scholars who specialize in:

- **Healing knowledge** (*fewus*, ፈውስ): herbal prescriptions, spiritual diagnoses
- **Magic-medical scrolls** (*tälsämat*, ጥልሰሞች): parchment talismans
- **Astrological computation** (*AwudeNegest*): zodiacal prescription matching
- **Liturgical chant** (*zema*) and sacred dance (*aquaquam*)

The term "Metsehafe Fewus" may refer to:
1. A **specific canonical scroll** attributed to Solomon or saints
2. A **genre** of Däbtära healing compendium (broader sense)
3. **Individual practitioner scrolls** personalized for a specific client (keyed to birth date, sign, and humoral profile)

## 3. Structure of a Metsehafe Fewus Scroll

A canonical Metsehafe Fewus scroll is organized into the following sections:

### 3.1 Introductory Invocation (ጸሎተ ፈውስ — Tselote Fewus)
Opening prayers calling upon God, the Holy Trinity, the Virgin Mary (ቅድስት
ማርያም), and protective saints (especially Saint Michael / ቅዱስ ሚካኤል) to
activate the healing power of the text.

### 3.2 Etiological Diagnosis Section (ምርመራ — Mirmera)
Lists possible **spiritual and humoral causes** of illness, organized by:
- **Yelubat** (ዓይነ-ልብ): diseases of the evil eye
- **Bärya** (ባርያ): possessive spirits causing abdominal/metabolic illness
- **Zar** (ዛር): spiritual forces requiring negotiation, not exorcism
- **Humoral imbalance**: excess of *Esat* (fire), *May* (water), *Afere* (earth), *Nifas* (air)

### 3.3 Prescription Tables (ፍቱሕ — Fituh)
Organized by body system and AwudeNegest zodiacal sign:
- **Herbal preparations** keyed to Ethiopian medicinal botany
- **Sacred timing**: which planetary hour, day, and moon phase to administer
- **Tsebel (ጸበል)** holy spring water for sacramental augmentation
- **Dietary instructions**: fasting protocols, food avoidances (antinutritional context)

### 3.4 Amulet Inscriptions (ጥልሰም — Tälsäm)
- Written in red and black ink on goatskin or sheepskin parchment
- Magic squares (*wifkir*, ውፍቅር), sacred numerological grids
- Protective names of angels and saints in Ge'ez numerological sequence

### 3.5 Closing Seal (ምሕረት ፊደሎቸ — Mihret Fideloche)
Closing prayer and numerological seal activating the scroll's efficacy,
typically including the practitioner's signature and the client's full name
(written for a specific individual — "written for [name]").

## 7. Bärya (ባርያ) — The Abdominal Disease Spirit

The **Bärya** is one of the most documented spiritual etiologies in Ethiopian
healing tradition. The McGill Library Ge'ez healing scroll (Archive.org identifier:
`McGillLibrary-osl_abyssinian-amulet_BibOsl7509-21406`) is specifically written
to combat Bärya:

> *"...against the terrible Bārya, a demon causing diseases of the belly,
> especially in women, and against other devils."*

**Domain A Debral Correlates** (strictly firewall-separated, for expert context only):
The condition called "Bärya" in the cultural tradition may Debrally correlate with
intestinal parasitosis, pelvic inflammatory disease, irritable bowel syndrome, or
functional dyspepsia. **Domain A Debral evaluation is always required independently.**
